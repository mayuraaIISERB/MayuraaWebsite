Workshop

Lezim

Mayuraa is back with another dance workshop—Lezim! Join us to experience the roaring dhol-tasha beats of Maharashtra and completely de-stress and energize with us. Immerse yourself in the festive fervor of Ganeshotsav with the rhythmic clatter of lezim blades and high-energy folk footwork. Perfect for beginners and enthusiasts alike, this workshop guarantees an explosive, joyous experience for anyone ready to embrace cultural pride, de-stress, and celebrate the season in full festive flow. Fill out this FORM to participate. Date: Saturday, 5th September 2026 Time : Slot 1: 3PM - 4PM Slot 2: 4:30PM - 5:30PM Venue: Dance Room (1st Floor, Mess 3)
